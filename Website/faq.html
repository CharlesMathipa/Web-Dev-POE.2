Frequently Asked Questions

What are the same-day delivery cutoff times?

Orders placed before 12:00 PM (Monday to Saturday) qualify for same-day afternoon delivery in major metropolitan zones.

What happens if the recipient is not at home?

Our driver will attempt to contact both the recipient and buyer. If unreachable, arrangements are safely left with building reception, security gates, or in a shaded water pack.

How do I keep my roses fresh for longer?

Trim 2cm from stem bottoms at a 45-degree angle every 2 days, replace water regularly, and avoid direct sunlight or heaters.

Can I send an anonymous gift?

Yes. Simply check the "Keep my name anonymous" checkbox during checkout, and your billing identity will be withheld from the recipient physical card.
